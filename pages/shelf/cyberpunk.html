Spheres float in front of and behind the lettering. Move the pointer near a sphere to deform its surface. Click or tap a sphere to break it into smaller beads. With this region focused, use the arrow keys to select a sphere, Enter or Space to pop it, and Home to reset. Metallic droplets grow from the spheres and detach upward. Reduce motion in the footer pauses the spheres, droplets, gradient and occasional lightning.
